Myself

About Me

I'm a Full-Stack Web Developer who started with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in 2024 and has since worked up to shipping production-ready apps with Next.js, React, Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB. I'm most drawn to the messy middle of a project — wiring authentication (Better Auth), untangling a hydration bug, or getting a same-origin cookie to actually work across two deployed domains — the kind of problem that only gets solved by reading the actual error, not guessing. I also fold AI tools like Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini directly into my workflow to move faster without cutting corners on the code I ship.

Away from the keyboard, I keep pigeons — there's something grounding about a hobby with no console output — and I'm usually a few chapters into a book. Both remind me to slow down, which turns out to be useful advice for debugging too.
